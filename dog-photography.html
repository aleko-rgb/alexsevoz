How it works

The anatomy of a jump.

A good flight shot isn't luck — it's knowing this particular dog's rhythm and hitting the shutter within a single frame. Four phases I track in every session.

01 / BEFORE

The approach

I watch the run-up, the paw rhythm, the favourite toy. I find the right angle and light before the dog even takes off.

02 / TAKEOFF

The launch

The fraction of a second where the back paws leave the ground. This is where the whole frame's composition gets decided.

03 / PEAK

The apex

The highest point of the jump — ears airborne, tail extended, full-body tension. This is the shot you came here for.

04 / LANDING

The return

Paws hit the ground, dust lifts off the grass. Sometimes the most honest frame happens right here, not at the peak.
