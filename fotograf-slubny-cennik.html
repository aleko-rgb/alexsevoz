Every couple I photograph gets the same level of care and attention, regardless of package. What changes is the time — and time is what lets me document the full arc of your day, from the quiet preparation to the last dance.

I work in a documentary style: close, calm, and unobtrusive. You won't spend your day posing. I'll give you a few simple directions during the couple's session — the rest is yours to live, and mine to photograph.

Below you'll find my packages for weddings in Poland. For destination weddings across Europe, get in touch — we'll build something that fits your plans.
